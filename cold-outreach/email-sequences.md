# Cold Email Sequences — Dental & Aesthetic Clinics

Two parallel sequences: one for dental clinics, one for aesthetic/med spa clinics.
Each is 3 steps. Total sequence length: ~10 days.

---

## Sequence Timing

```
Day 0  → Step 1: Initial outreach
Day 3  → Step 2: Follow-up (no reply received)
Day 7  → Step 3: Final touch / value + close
Day 10 → Mark as "sequence complete" — do not email again for 90 days
```

---

## DENTAL CLINIC SEQUENCE

---

### Step 1 — Initial Outreach

**Subject options (A/B test one per week):**
- `Quick question for {{first_name}}`
- `{{clinic_name}} — had a thought`
- `Noticed something about {{clinic_name}}`

**Body:**

```
Hi {{first_name}},

I was looking at {{clinic_name}} on Google Maps and noticed 
{{personalization_tag}} — I work with dental practices on exactly that.

I help small dental offices attract more local patients without 
spending money on ads — usually through optimizing their Google 
Business profile and getting a steady flow of patient referrals 
set up systematically.

Most practices I work with pick up 3–8 new patients/month within 
the first 60 days.

Would a 15-minute call this week make sense to see if it's a fit?

{{sender_name}}
{{sender_title}}
{{sender_phone}}
```

**Personalization tag examples:**
- `you're getting solid reviews but aren't ranking in the top 3 local results`
- `your photos look a bit dated — patients judge that before they even call`
- `you don't seem to have a referral program listed anywhere`
- `you're closed on Fridays, which a lot of practices are opening up`

---

### Step 2 — Follow-Up 1 (Day 3)

**Subject:** `Re: Quick question for {{first_name}}`  
*(Reply to same thread — keeps it in context)*

**Body:**

```
Hi {{first_name}},

Just bumping this up — I know inboxes get busy.

One thing I've seen work really well for practices like {{clinic_name}}: 
setting up a simple "refer a friend" system that your front desk 
can run in 5 minutes/day. No app, no tech. Just a process.

The practices that do this consistently see 2–3 extra new patients 
per month from existing patients alone.

Happy to walk you through it on a quick call — no pitch, just 
want to show you the framework.

Worth 15 minutes?

{{sender_name}}
```

---

### Step 3 — Final Touch (Day 7)

**Subject:** `Last note — {{clinic_name}}`

**Body:**

```
Hi {{first_name}},

I'll keep this short — last email from me on this.

I put together a quick overview of what I've seen work for 
dental practices in {{city}} specifically. Happy to send it 
over if useful (it's a one-pager, no fluff).

If timing isn't right, totally understand. I'll check back 
in a few months.

Either way — good luck with {{clinic_name}}. The reviews 
look great.

{{sender_name}}

P.S. If you ever want that one-pager, just reply "send it" 
and I'll get it to you.
```

## AESTHETIC / MED SPA SEQUENCE

---

### Step 1 — Initial Outreach

**Subject options:**
- `Quick idea for {{clinic_name}}`
- `{{first_name}} — noticed this about your clinic`
- `Thought this might be relevant for {{clinic_name}}`

**Body:**

```
Hi {{first_name}},

I came across {{clinic_name}} while researching aesthetic 
clinics in {{city}} — {{personalization_tag}}.

I work with small med spas and aesthetic practices on 
client retention and new patient acquisition — without 
paid ads. Specifically: getting existing clients to come 
back more often and refer friends automatically.

The average practice I work with adds $3–5K/month in 
recurring revenue within 90 days just from retention fixes.

Would a quick 15-minute call make sense to see if there's 
a fit for your practice?

{{sender_name}}
{{sender_title}}
{{sender_phone}}
```

**Personalization tag examples:**
- `you have great before/after results but I couldn't find a loyalty program mentioned anywhere`
- `you're only on Instagram but not showing up in local Google searches at all`
- `your booking page is a bit buried — most clients probably drop off before they find it`
- `you offer a great range of services but there's no obvious "starter package" for new patients`

---

### Step 2 — Follow-Up 1 (Day 3)

**Subject:** `Re: Quick idea for {{clinic_name}}`

**Body:**

```
Hi {{first_name}},

Following up in case this got buried.

One quick stat that might be relevant: aesthetic clinics 
typically see 60–70% of revenue come from repeat clients — 
but most don't have a system to bring them back predictably.

I've helped a few practices set up a simple reactivation 
sequence (email + text) that brings back dormant clients 
almost on autopilot. Takes about a day to set up.

If that sounds interesting, I'd love to show you how it 
works on a short call.

{{sender_name}}
```

---

### Step 3 — Final Touch (Day 7)

**Subject:** `Closing the loop — {{clinic_name}}`

**Body:**

```
Hi {{first_name}},

Last note from me — don't want to clutter your inbox.

I wrote up a short breakdown of the 3 things aesthetic 
clinics in {{city}} are doing right now to grow without 
ads. Happy to send it over if you're curious.

No strings. Just figured it might be useful given what 
{{clinic_name}} is building.

If now isn't the right time, no worries at all — I'll 
circle back later in the year.

{{sender_name}}

P.S. Reply "yes" if you want the breakdown — I'll send 
it right over.
```

## Subject Line Rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Keep under 40 characters | Mobile preview shows ~40 chars |
| No ALL CAPS | Spam trigger |
| No exclamation marks in subject | Spam trigger |
| No "FREE", "guarantee", "limited time" | Hard spam words |
| Use the prospect's name or clinic name | Increases open rate by ~20% |
| "Re:" prefix on follow-ups | Groups thread, feels conversational |

---

## Copy Rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| First sentence must reference them specifically | Proves it's not a blast |
| Email body ≤ 150 words | Respect their time |
| One call to action only | Two CTAs = zero CTAs |
| Plain text only (no HTML, no images, no tracking pixels) | Better deliverability, feels human |
| No attachments in cold email | Immediate spam trigger |
| Sign off with first name only on follow-ups | Feels warmer, less formal |

---

## A/B Testing Plan

Test one variable at a time. Run each variant for 2 weeks minimum.

| Week | Variable Testing |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Subject line A vs B |
| 3–4 | Opening sentence angle |
| 5–6 | CTA phrasing (call vs email vs "worth chatting?") |
| 7–8 | Email length (100 words vs 150 words) |

Track in Sheets: which variant → which lead → did they reply.
