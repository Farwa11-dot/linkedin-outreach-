# Daily Operating Routine

One clear routine. Do it every weekday. Takes 45–60 minutes total.
Results compound over weeks, not days.

---

## The Full Day at a Glance

```
8:30am  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
        │  MORNING BLOCK (~20 min)                    │
        │  Handle follow-ups and replies               │
        └─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

9:00am  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
        │  OUTREACH BLOCK (~30 min)                   │
        │  Research new leads + send initial emails    │
        └─────────────────────────────────────────────┘

End of  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  day   │  CLOSE-OUT (5 min)                          │
        │  Log replies, update CRM, set next steps     │
        └─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## Morning Block — 20 Minutes

### Step 1: Check for Replies (5 min)

Open Gmail. Search: `in:inbox -in:spam`

For each reply in your outreach threads:
- [ ] Read it fully — don't skim
- [ ] Open CRM → find the lead → check `Reply Received`, fill in `Reply Date`
- [ ] Write a one-sentence `Reply Summary` in column V
- [ ] Set `Status` = `Replied` (or appropriate stage)
- [ ] Clear the `Next Follow-Up Date` (stop the sequence)
- [ ] Draft your response — **do not send yet** (see below)

**Draft rule:** Write your reply, then wait 5 minutes and re-read it before sending.
This catches reactive responses. Replies deserve thoughtful answers.

### Step 2: Open Follow-Up Queue (3 min)

Open Google Sheets → `Follow-Up Queue` tab.

Read through today's list. For each lead:
- [ ] Note their name, business, and what step this is
- [ ] Click to Gmail, find the original thread
- [ ] Read what you sent them — understand the full context before writing again

You are not opening Gmail and firing off emails. You are preparing.

### Step 3: Send Follow-Ups (12 min)

For each lead in the queue (aim for max 5–8 follow-ups per morning):

```
1. Open the right email template from your Templates tab
2. Paste into Gmail → Reply to original thread
3. Personalize — at minimum: their first name, clinic name
4. Read it out loud — does it sound human?
5. Send
6. Immediately: update CRM status, dates, Next Follow-Up Date
7. Log in Call Log
```

**If the queue has more than 8 leads:** Prioritize by stage.
Handle Step 2 and Step 3 follow-ups first (closer to a decision).
Step 4 final emails can wait until afternoon.

**If a follow-up email doesn't feel right:** Don't send it. Add a note in
column Y, set `Next Follow-Up Date` = tomorrow, and rewrite it fresh then.
A delayed follow-up beats a bad one.

## Outreach Block — 30 Minutes

### Step 4: Research New Leads (15 min)

Open Google Maps. Search for clinics in your target city:
- `dentist near [city]`
- `dental clinic [city, state]`
- `med spa [city]`
- `aesthetic clinic [city]`

For each result, spend 2–3 minutes:

```
[ ] Is this a real business? (not a chain, not a hospital system)
[ ] Can I find a direct email? (website About/Contact page)
[ ] What is the one specific thing I noticed? (personalization note)
[ ] Is this email already in my CRM? (check with Ctrl+F before adding)
```

Add 5–10 leads to the CRM Leads tab. Fill in every column through
`Personalization Note`. Leave all date/status columns blank.

Set Status = `New Lead`. You will email them in Step 5.

**Stopping rule:** If you cannot find a specific personalization note
for a lead after 3 minutes of looking, skip them. You can come back.
Do not email without one.

### Step 5: Send Initial Emails (15 min)

From your fresh leads (and any `New Lead` status leads from previous days):

```
1. Open Templates tab → copy Step 1 body for the right industry
2. New Gmail → compose
3. Fill in: To, Subject
4. Paste body
5. Replace all {{placeholders}} — first name, clinic name, city, personalization note
6. Read it as if you received it. Would you reply? If no, rewrite.
7. Send
8. Immediately: CRM → Status = "Contacted", Initial Email Date = today,
   Next Follow-Up Date = today + 2
9. Log in Call Log: Action = "Email Sent", Step = "Initial"
```

**Batch size:** 5–10 initial emails per day is the right volume for quality outreach.
At this rate, you'll contact 100–200 new leads per month — enough to
generate real conversations without sacrificing personalization.

**Do not rush this block.** If you're copying and pasting without editing,
you're sending a blast. Stop, slow down, and personalize.

---

## Close-Out — 5 Minutes

At the end of the workday:

```
[ ] Any late replies in Gmail? Log them in CRM.
[ ] Any pending CRM updates you forgot this morning? Do them now.
[ ] Is tomorrow's Follow-Up Queue ready?
    Open Sheets → Follow-Up Queue → count tomorrow's leads
    If 10+, note it — plan to start morning block early
[ ] Any leads you need to prep before emailing tomorrow?
    Add a note in column Y: "Research X before emailing"
```

## Weekly Rhythm

### Monday
- Review the previous week's stats (10 min)
  - How many initial emails sent?
  - How many replies?
  - How many meetings booked?
  - What patterns do you see? (Which subject lines got replies? Which personalization angles resonated?)
- Set the week's target: how many new leads to contact this week?

### Wednesday
- Mid-week pulse check (5 min)
  - Is the Follow-Up Queue manageable?
  - Any leads stuck in a stage longer than expected?
  - Any A/B subject line test conclusions?

### Friday
- Log all final updates in CRM (5 min)
- Park any no-reply sequences that completed this week
- Write down 1–2 things you'll do differently next week

## Tracking Your Numbers

Keep a simple weekly log (in a Notes column or a separate tab):

| Week | New Leads Added | Initial Emails Sent | Replies Received | Calls Booked | Reply Rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Apr 7 | 35 | 28 | 4 | 1 | 14.3% |
| Apr 14 | 40 | 35 | 6 | 2 | 17.1% |

**What good looks like in months 1–3:**
- Reply rate: 5–15% (varies heavily by angle and personalization quality)
- Meeting rate: 20–40% of replies
- Booking rate: 30–60% of meetings

**If reply rate < 3% after 50 sends:** The copy needs work, not the volume.
Rewrite Step 1. Change the personalization angle. Try a different subject line.

**If reply rate > 15%:** You've found a strong angle. Dig deeper into it.

---

## Energy Management

Cold outreach is repetitive work. It degrades in quality when you're tired.

**Do the outreach block at your best time of day.** If you're sharpest in
the morning — protect that time for research and writing, not admin.

**Do not batch more than 10 initial emails in one session.** Quality
drops sharply after that. Better to do two 5-email sessions than
one 12-email session where emails 8–12 are copy-paste with names swapped.

**Celebrate replies, not sends.** Sends are inputs. Replies are outcomes.
End each day by counting replies, not emails sent.

---

## When You're Behind

Life happens. If you miss 3 days and your Follow-Up Queue has 20 leads:

1. Do NOT blast all 20 at once
2. Prioritize: handle Step 3 and Step 4 follow-ups first (they've been waiting longest)
3. Do 8–10 per day over 2–3 days to clear the queue
4. New lead outreach pauses until the queue is manageable again

**It is better to be consistent with 5 touchpoints/day than
to sprint with 50 once a week and then disappear.**
