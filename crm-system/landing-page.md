# Simple Landing Page / Demo Page

A single web page that does one job: convert a curious prospect into
a booked call. Not a marketing site. Not a portfolio. One page, one CTA.

You do not need this on Day 1. Build it in Week 2–3 when you've had
your first few conversations and know what questions come up most.
Before that, your emails and calls carry the weight.

---

## What This Page Is For

- Prospects who ask "can you send me something to look at?"
- Your email signature link (optional, subtle)
- After a positive DM conversation when they want more context
- As the destination when someone finds you through referral

**What it is NOT for:** Cold traffic, ads, SEO. You are not building
a marketing site. This is a credibility asset for warm leads only.

## Page Structure — The Only 6 Sections You Need

```
[SECTION 1] Headline + Subheadline
[SECTION 2] The Problem (1–2 sentences)
[SECTION 3] How It Works (3 steps, icons optional)
[SECTION 4] What They Get (3 bullet points)
[SECTION 5] Social Proof (1 specific result, real or directional)
[SECTION 6] CTA — Book a 10-min call (Calendly embed or link)
```

Total reading time: 90 seconds. That's all you want. They're not
here to read — they're here to decide if they should book the call.

---

## Section-by-Section Copy

### Section 1 — Headline + Subheadline

**Headline:**
```
Stop Losing Patient Bookings to Missed Calls
```

**Subheadline:**
```
A 24/7 answering system for dental and aesthetic clinics that captures
every patient inquiry — day or night — and books them directly into your calendar.
```

**Rules:**
- Headline = the problem they already feel, not a feature
- Subheadline = one sentence explaining what you do and who it's for
- No jargon. No "AI-powered." No "revolutionary." Plain language only.

---

### Section 2 — The Problem

```
Every time a patient calls after hours and hits voicemail, there's a 
67% chance they call the next practice on Google instead.

For a cosmetic dental practice, that's $3,000–$8,000 walking out the door.
Per call.
```

Use real numbers. Specific claims convert better than vague ones.
If you don't have your own data yet, use directional estimates based
on industry benchmarks (the above numbers are well-supported).

---

### Section 3 — How It Works

Three steps. Icons optional (Carrd has built-in icon support).

```
Step 1 — Every call answered
Your practice number stays the same. Every call — at any hour —
is answered with a professional greeting.

Step 2 — Patient qualified and booked
The system identifies why they're calling, collects what it needs,
and schedules their appointment directly into your calendar.

Step 3 — Your front desk sees everything
Every call logged, every booking confirmed, every message flagged —
waiting for your team when they open up in the morning.
```

---

### Section 4 — What They Get

Three concrete outcomes. No feature list.

```
✓ Never miss an after-hours or weekend patient inquiry again
✓ New patient bookings confirmed while you sleep
✓ Your front desk starts each day with appointments already filled, not calls to return
```

---

### Section 5 — Social Proof

If you have a real client result:
```
"In the first 60 days, we recovered 11 new patient bookings that would
have gone to voicemail. At $900 average patient value, that's roughly
$9,900 in revenue we would have lost."
— [Practice Name or "General Dental Practice, Phoenix AZ"]
```

If you don't have a client yet, use a directional proof point:
```
Dental practices that answer every call convert new patient inquiries
at 3× the rate of practices that use voicemail alone.
— Based on analysis of 50+ single-location dental practices
```

Do not fabricate testimonials. If you have no proof yet, replace this section
with a brief statement about your process:
```
Every setup includes a live test call before going live —
so you see exactly what your patients will experience before the system handles a real call.
```

---

### Section 6 — CTA

**Primary CTA button:**
```
Book a Free 10-Min Intro Call
```

Link this to your Calendly booking page.

**Below the button, in smaller text:**
```
No pitch. No pressure. We'll figure out together if it makes sense for your practice.
If it doesn't, I'll tell you.
```

This secondary line removes the fear of being sold to — which is the
primary reason people don't click booking links.

## Building It in Carrd (25 Minutes)

### Step 1 — Create Account (3 min)
1. Go to [carrd.co](https://carrd.co) → Get Started Free
2. Sign up with Google or email
3. Choose a template: **Basic** (simplest), or **Pitch** (pre-structured)

### Step 2 — Set Up Structure (5 min)
1. Delete all default sections
2. Add sections in order: Container → Text (headline) → Text (problem) →
   Text (how it works) → Text (what they get) → Text (proof) → Button (CTA)
3. Set background: white or very light gray (#f8f8f8)
4. Font: Inter or Lato (both clean, readable, professional)

### Step 3 — Fill in Copy (10 min)
Paste your headline, subheadline, problem, 3 steps, 3 bullets, proof, CTA.
Adjust spacing. Preview on mobile view — most visitors will be on their phone.

### Step 4 — Add CTA Button (3 min)
Add a Button element → set URL to your Calendly link
Button label: `Book a Free 10-Min Call`
Button color: dark navy (#1a2e4a) or whatever matches your brand

### Step 5 — Publish (2 min)
1. Site Settings → Site name: `[yourname]-clinics` or similar
2. Click **Publish**
3. Your URL: `yourname-clinics.carrd.co`

For a custom domain ($19/year on Carrd Pro Lite):
`yourbusiness.com` or `clinicgrowth.io` — short, professional, memorable.

### Step 6 — Test on Mobile (2 min)
Open the published URL on your phone.
Does the headline fit without wrapping awkwardly?
Is the CTA button easy to tap?
Does it load in under 2 seconds?

Fix anything that breaks on mobile. Most clinic owners will view this
on their phone between patients.

## What to Include in the Carrd Embed (If Using Calendly Inline)

Instead of a button linking to Calendly, embed the calendar directly:

1. Calendly → Event Type → Share → Add to Website → copy embed code
2. In Carrd: Add element → **Embed** → paste the code
3. Set height to 600px minimum

This removes one click from the booking flow — they see the calendar
directly on the page and pick a time immediately.

## Google Sites Alternative (Truly Free, No Expiry)

If you don't want to use Carrd:

1. Go to [sites.google.com](https://sites.google.com) → Blank
2. Click the title area → type your headline
3. Add sections: **Text box, Text box, Text box, Button, Embed (Calendly)**
4. Publish → set URL: `sites.google.com/view/yourname-clinics`
5. Share this URL

Google Sites is free forever with no limitations. The design is more basic
but it's professional enough for a warm prospect who wants to learn more.
The URL (`sites.google.com/view/...`) is longer and less memorable —
worth the $19 to get a real domain eventually.

## When to Send the Page Link

| Situation | Send? |
|---|---|
| Cold email Step 1 | Never — too early, signals mass outreach |
| After they ask "can you send more info?" | Yes — with a specific question first |
| After a positive call conversation | Yes — as follow-up, with Calendly pre-loaded |
| In your email signature | Yes — subtle link only |
| After they no-show a call | Yes — "here's what we would have covered" |
| As a DM first message | Never |

Keep it as a warm-lead tool, not a top-of-funnel one.
