# Daily Lead Sourcing System — 10–20 New Leads in 30 Minutes

A repeatable, free process for finding and logging qualified leads every weekday.
Runs in parallel with your outreach block. Takes 30 minutes. Generates
100–400 research-ready leads per month.

---

## The 30-Minute Daily Sourcing Block

```
Minutes 0–5    Pick today's city + search terms
Minutes 5–20   Google Maps sweep — find 15–25 listings
Minutes 20–28  Email verification — find direct emails for 10–15
Minutes 28–30  Log to CRM — set status, write personalization note
```

Run this block every afternoon after your morning outreach.
New leads logged today go into the queue for tomorrow's initial emails.

- Higher population growth = more new clinic openings
- More aesthetics-focused culture (higher spend on appearance)
- Competitive dental markets where practices are more growth-minded

## Step 2 — Google Maps Search Playbook

### The 8 Search Terms

Use one search term per sourcing session. Rotate through them:

| # | Search Term | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `dentist [city] [state]` | General dental practices |
| 2 | `cosmetic dentist [city]` | Higher-ticket dental |
| 3 | `dental implants [city]` | Highest-ticket dental |
| 4 | `orthodontist [city]` | Invisalign, braces |
| 5 | `med spa [city]` | Aesthetics — general |
| 6 | `botox [city]` | Injectables — high intent |
| 7 | `aesthetic clinic [city]` | Broader aesthetics |
| 8 | `laser hair removal [city]` | Service-specific aesthetics |

**Tip:** Use Google Maps search (not Google Search). Maps shows the
full business panel with hours, reviews, photos, and website link —
all the data you need for scoring without visiting multiple pages.

### How to Work the Map

1. Open Google Maps in Chrome
2. Search: `cosmetic dentist Austin TX`
3. Maps will show a list on the left + pins on the right
4. **Work the list panel, not the map** — the list is faster
5. For each result:
   - Does it have a website? (no website = probably low score)
   - How many reviews? (under 30 → potential urgency signal)
   - What's the rating? (3.8–4.3 → sweet spot for our offer)
   - Is there a "Book" or "Reserve" button? (no button → urgency signal)
   - What are the hours? (closes early or no weekends → urgency signal)
6. Click into listings that look promising → copy the website URL

**Skim pace:** You should be able to evaluate each listing in 15–20 seconds.
Do not read reviews at this stage. You're filtering, not researching.

### Scroll and Zoom Strategy

Google Maps shows 20 results per search but there are often 50–100+.
To get more:
- Scroll to the bottom of the list → "Load more"
- Zoom the map to a specific neighborhood → listings refresh for that area
- Try different spelling: "dentist office" vs "dental clinic" vs "dental practice"

In a city like Miami or Dallas you can find 80–120 practices per search term.
You don't need all of them — stop at 20–25 that pass the visual filter.

## Step 3 — Email Finding (Free Methods)

For each practice that passes the visual filter, find a direct email.
Work through these methods in order. Stop when you have an email.

### Method 1 — Their Website (1 minute)

Go to their website. Check these pages in order:
1. Contact page
2. About page (often has doctor's email or office manager)
3. Footer (many practices put `info@` or a direct email here)
4. Team page (sometimes individual emails per staff member)

**What you're looking for:**
- `dr.smith@clinicname.com` — owner direct (best, score 1pt in Contact)
- `manager@clinicname.com` — decision-maker adjacent (good)
- `hello@clinicname.com` or `contact@clinicname.com` — generic but workable
- `info@clinicname.com` — generic, score 0.25pt in Contact

### Method 2 — Hunter.io (Free tier: 25 searches/month)

1. Go to [hunter.io](https://hunter.io)
2. Enter their domain (e.g., `smilesdental.com`)
3. Hunter shows all emails found at that domain + confidence score
4. Use any email with > 70% confidence

**Free tier strategy:** Save your 25 Hunter searches for practices
where you can't find an email on the website but it's a HIGH score lead.
Don't waste Hunter credits on 4/10 leads.

### Method 3 — Google Search Operators

```
"@smilesdental.com" contact
site:smilesdental.com email
"smilesdental.com" "@" email
"dr" "@smilesdental.com"
```

These surface email addresses indexed in Google — often from old
directory listings, PDF contact sheets, or their own blog posts.

### Method 4 — Google Maps Q&A and Owner Posts

Some practice owners post contact info directly in Google Maps
Q&A sections or in their owner responses to reviews.
Check the Q&A tab on their Maps listing — takes 10 seconds.

### Method 5 — Contact Form Only

If they have no email and only a contact form:
- Log the practice with `Contact Source = Website (form only)`
- Note in the email field: `contact form at [URL]`
- Score `Contact Quality = 0` (form only)
- Only email if their score is still 6+ on other dimensions

You can use the form for Step 1. It's not ideal but it works
for high-score leads with no other option.

## Step 4 — Quick Scoring (2 Minutes Per Lead)

While you have the listing open, score immediately — don't wait.

Use the 5-dimension model from `lead-scoring.md`:

```
Ads:      0–2  (check Google search for their name in sponsored results)
Service:  0–2  (read their specialties/services from Maps listing)
Urgency:  0–3  (count: low reviews, no booking, early closing, no responses)
Website:  0–2  (glance at mobile version of their site)
Contact:  0–1  (direct email vs generic)

Total: ___/10 → Priority Tier
```

**Fast scoring shortcuts:**
- If they're in the top 3 Google Maps results → probably lower urgency (good reviews, booking button)
- If they're results 4–10 → more likely to have gaps
- If reviews are 4.0–4.4 with < 40 reviews → almost always score 6+ on urgency alone

**Drop leads immediately if:**
- Score < 4 (not worth the email time)
- No email found and contact form only + score < 7
- Chain/corporate practice (Aspen Dental, Heartland, chain med spa)
- No website at all AND no Google presence (too small/dormant)

## Step 5 — Logging to CRM (60 Seconds Per Lead)

Add each lead to the `Leads` tab:

1. **Lead ID:** Next available (L042, L043, etc.)
2. **Business Name, Industry, City, State:** Copy from Maps
3. **Contact Name + First Name:** From website About page (or "Owner" if unknown)
4. **Email:** What you found
5. **Website:** Their URL
6. **Contact Source:** `Google Maps`
7. **Personalization Note:** Write ONE specific observation RIGHT NOW while it's fresh
   - Bad: "Dental practice in Austin"
   - Good: "No booking button, closes at 4pm, 14 reviews with zero responses"
8. **Score columns M–Q:** Fill in your quick scores
9. **Lead Score + Priority Tier:** Auto-calculate from formulas
10. **Status:** `New Lead`

**Do NOT fill in any date columns yet.** Those get filled when you email.

---

## Personalization Note — Write It Now, Not Later

The single biggest mistake in lead sourcing is logging the business
without the observation and planning to write it later.

By the time you sit down to email them tomorrow, you won't remember
what was specific about their listing. You'll write something generic.
Generic emails don't get replied to.

**The 20-second rule:** Before closing the Maps listing, write one
specific thing you noticed. In the Notes column. Right now.

Examples of 20-second observations:
```
"4.1 stars, 22 reviews, 0 responses to any review, no booking button"
"Closed Saturdays, no after-hours message listed"
"Active Instagram (checked quickly) but Google Business has no posts in 4 months"
"Website is broken on mobile — checked in Chrome DevTools"
"Running Google Ads (appeared in sponsored results for 'dentist Austin')"
```

## Quality Control — What Goes In vs What Gets Skipped

**Goes in the CRM:**
- Score ≥ 4
- Email found (website email, Hunter, or contact form on high-score lead)
- Independent practice (not a chain/franchise)
- Has at least one visible urgency signal

**Gets skipped:**
- Score < 4 after quick check
- Chain or franchise (Aspen Dental, Heartland, Ideal Image, etc.)
- No website, no Google presence, no contact info anywhere
- Already in your CRM (check Ctrl+F before logging)
- Hospital-based dental or dermatology (too institutional, wrong buyer)

## City Exhaustion — When to Move On

A city is "worked" when:
- You've sourced 50+ leads from it in the last 90 days
- The Maps results are mostly repeating (same practices appearing again)
- You're scoring < 10 leads per search term

Move to the next city in your queue. Return to worked cities after 90 days —
new practices will have opened, old ones will have changed.
