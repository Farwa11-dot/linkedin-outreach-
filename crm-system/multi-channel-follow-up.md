# Multi-Channel Follow-Up System

Email alone leaves conversions on the table. Different people live in
different channels. This system adds phone and DM touchpoints at the
right moments — not randomly, but triggered by lead tier and silence.

---

## Channel Strategy by Lead Tier

| Tier | Channels Used | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 HOT (8–10) | Email + Phone (Day 5) + DM (Day 7) | High value — exhaust every channel before parking |
| 🟠 WARM (5.5–7.5) | Email + DM (Day 7) | Standard sequence with one DM touchpoint |
| 🟡 COOL (3–5) | Email only | Not worth multi-channel investment |
| ⚪ COLD (0–2.5) | None until rescored | Save your time |

---

## HOT Lead Sequence (Days 0–11)

The most important sequence in the system. Every day matters.

```
Day 0   EMAIL     Step 1 — Initial outreach (missed calls angle)
Day 2   EMAIL     Follow-Up 1 — different angle, one proof point
Day 4   EMAIL     Follow-Up 2 — value frame, one clear CTA
Day 5   PHONE     Call attempt (HOT leads only)
Day 5   EMAIL     Voicemail follow-up (if no answer on phone)
Day 7   DM        Instagram or Facebook — brief, warm, no pitch
Day 10  EMAIL     Breakup email — short, genuine, door open
Day 11  PARK      Status → Park - 90 Days
```

### Day 0 — Email (Step 1)

Initial outreach. See `email-sequences.md` for full templates.
Subject: personal, lowercase, references the clinic by name.

---

### Day 2 — Email (Follow-Up 1)

Reply to original thread. Different angle — social proof or question:

```
Subject: Re: [original subject]

Hi {{first_name}},

Quick follow-up — a dental practice in {{state}} was missing about
40 calls a month after hours. We set up a system to catch those.
In 60 days: 11 recovered bookings.

Worth a 10-minute look to see if the numbers make sense for
{{clinic_name}}?

[Your name]
```

---

### Day 4 — Email (Follow-Up 2)

```
Subject: Re: [original subject]

Hi {{first_name}},

One more thought —

The practices that handle this well do one thing differently:
they never let a call go unanswered, even at 9pm.

Not complicated. Just consistent.

If {{clinic_name}} wants to see what that looks like in practice —
15 minutes, I'll show you exactly what's being caught.

[Your name]
```

---

### Day 5 — Phone Call (HOT Leads Only)

Make the call between 10am–12pm or 2pm–4pm in their local timezone.
Avoid Monday mornings and Friday afternoons.

**Call script:**
```
"Hi, is this [contact name]? This is [your name].

I sent you a couple of emails about [clinic name] this week —
wanted to make sure they didn't get lost.

I help dental practices capture bookings that slip through missed
calls. Do you have 2 minutes now or is there a better time today?"
```

**If they answer and engage:** run compressed discovery (3 questions),
book the call before you hang up.

**If voicemail:** 20 seconds max, then immediately send the voicemail follow-up email.

**Voicemail:**
```
"Hi [name], this is [your name]. I sent a couple of emails about
[clinic name] this week. My number is [number]. Happy to connect
here or by email — talk soon."
```

**Voicemail follow-up email (send within 2 minutes of leaving voicemail):**
```
Subject: Re: [original thread]

Hi {{first_name}},

Just left you a voicemail — wanted to make sure my emails got through.

If easier to reply here: how does {{clinic_name}} handle after-hours
patient calls? That's all I want to know to start.

My number is [number] if a call is easier.

[Your name]
```

---

### Day 7 — DM (Instagram or Facebook)

Find their business page (most dental and aesthetic clinics have Instagram).
Message from your personal profile or a professional business profile.
Do not message from an obviously automated or brand-new account.

**Find the page:** Search the clinic name on Instagram or Facebook.
Check if they have a "Message" button on their business page.

**DM message:**
```
Hey [first name] — I sent a couple of emails to [clinic name]
this week (might have landed in spam).

Quick version: I work with dental practices on capturing the patient
bookings that slip through missed calls. Worth a 10-min look?
```

Keep it under 4 lines. No links. No images. No emojis.

**If they respond on DM:** Have the conversation there, but when booking
a call, move to email confirmation ("Let me send you a confirmation
to your email so we both have it").

**If they don't respond to DM within 48h:** Note in CRM "DM sent, no response."
Move to Day 10 breakup email.

---

### Day 10 — Email (Breakup)

```
Subject: closing the loop — {{clinic_name}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Last one from me — I don't want to clutter your inbox.

If the timing ever changes and you want to look at the missed
call problem at {{clinic_name}}, I'm one email away.

Good luck with the practice.

[Your name]
```

---

### Day 11 — Park

Status → `Park - 90 Days`. Next Follow-Up Date → today + 90.
Notes → "Completed full HOT sequence (email×4 + phone + DM). Park until [date]."

## WARM Lead Sequence (Days 0–10)

```
Day 0   EMAIL     Step 1
Day 3   EMAIL     Follow-Up 1
Day 6   EMAIL     Follow-Up 2
Day 7   DM        Instagram or Facebook (optional — skip if no active page)
Day 10  EMAIL     Breakup email
Day 11  PARK      Park - 90 Days
```

No phone call for WARM leads unless they reply positively and request one.
DM on Day 7 is optional — only if their Instagram/Facebook page is active
(posts in the last 30 days). No point DMing a dead account.

## COOL Lead Sequence (Days 0–14)

Email only. Slightly longer spacing.

```
Day 0   EMAIL     Step 1
Day 4   EMAIL     Follow-Up 1
Day 8   EMAIL     Follow-Up 2
Day 14  EMAIL     Breakup email
Day 15  PARK      Park - 90 Days
```

## When to Escalate to Phone

### Always call:
- HOT lead (8+) that hasn't replied after 2 emails (Day 5)
- Lead that replied positively, agreed to a call, then went silent (same day)
- Lead that no-showed a booked call (same day, within 1 hour of missed call)

### Never cold call:
- WARM or COOL leads who haven't replied to emails
- Leads who said "not now" via email (wait for their date)
- Leads who unsubscribed (never contact again via any channel)

### The 3-call rule:
Maximum 3 call attempts per lead, across the entire sequence.
If all three hit voicemail with no callback, the phone channel is exhausted.
Move to email breakup only.

## Multi-Channel Timing Rules

| Rule | Reason |
|---|---|
| Never call and email the same day (except voicemail follow-up) | Looks desperate |
| DM comes after 2+ emails, never before | Earn the DM channel with prior outreach |
| Phone before DM in HOT sequence | Phone shows higher intent; DM is the last resort |
| Never DM and email on the same day | One channel per day |
| Park after sequence is complete — never restart the same sequence | Restarting signals you don't track your own contacts |

## The Reactivation Sequence (90 Days Later)

When a parked lead's reactivation date arrives, do not restart the original sequence.
Write a completely fresh email that acknowledges time has passed.

```
Subject: checking back in — {{clinic_name}}

Hi {{first_name}},

I reached out a few months ago about the missed call problem
at {{clinic_name}} — you mentioned the timing wasn't right.

Checking back in to see if things have changed.

Still helping practices in [city] capture the patient bookings
they're currently losing after hours. Happy to show you what
that looks like in 10 minutes if the timing is better now.

[Your name]
```

If no reply to the reactivation email within 7 days → Status → `Lost`.
Do not run a full sequence again. One shot at reactivation.
