# Offer Structure & Pricing

Three offers. One conversation flow. Structured so you can close
on the first call without a follow-up "let me send you a proposal."

---

## The Offer Architecture

```
ENTRY OFFER ──────► MAIN OFFER ──────► UPGRADE
(low friction)      (core product)      (optional)

Missed Call Audit   AI Receptionist     Full Setup +
  [Free / $0]       System              White-Glove
                    [$X/month]          [$Y/month]
```

The Entry Offer exists to get undecided leads on the phone and into
your ecosystem. The Main Offer is what you actually sell. The Upgrade
is for clients who want you to handle everything.

---

## Offer 1 — Entry Offer: The Missed Call Audit

### What It Is

A free, 10-minute analysis of how many calls the practice is likely
missing each week, and what that costs them in lost revenue.

You run it live during the first call. No prep required. No deliverable.
It's a conversation tool, not a product.

### Why It Works

- Zero risk for the prospect (free, no commitment)
- Forces them to calculate their own loss before hearing your price
- Creates permission to present the solution naturally

### How to Position It

**In email (Step 3 — value email):**
```
"I put together a quick framework for measuring missed call cost
for practices the size of {{clinic_name}}. Happy to run through
it with you in about 10 minutes — no charge, no commitment.
If the numbers don't make sense for you, I'll tell you honestly."
```

**On a call (when they're hesitant about the main offer):**
```
"Before we talk about anything else — let me just show you
the audit. It takes 10 minutes. If the number we arrive at
is too small to matter, we shake hands and move on.
But most practices are surprised by it."
```

### The Audit — Run Live on the Call

Ask these three questions and do the math with them:

**Q1:** "How many calls would you guess your practice gets in a typical week?"
(They answer: let's say 80)

**Q2:** "What percentage would you estimate are missed — voicemail, during busy periods, after hours?"
(They answer: "Maybe 15–20%")

**Math:** 80 × 18% = ~14 missed calls/week × 4 weeks = ~56/month

**Q3:** "Of those missed calls, what percentage were likely new patients looking to book?"
(They answer: "Probably half")

**Math:** 56 × 50% = 28 lost new patient inquiries/month

**Q4:** "And your average new patient value — first visit plus follow-up — is roughly what?"
(They say: "$900")

**Math:** 28 × $900 × 20% booking rate = **$5,040/month in recovered revenue potential**

Show them this math on your screen or talk through it.
Then: "That's the number we're trying to recover. Our system costs
well under 10% of that. Want to see how it works?"

## Offer 2 — Main Offer: AI Receptionist System

### What It Is

A 24/7 automated phone answering and booking system that:
- Answers every call, day and night
- Qualifies the caller (new patient? existing? emergency?)
- Books appointments directly into the practice calendar
- Sends confirmation SMS to the patient
- Logs everything for the front desk to review in the morning

### Pricing Model

**Structure:** Monthly retainer, no long-term contract.

| Tier | Price | Call Volume | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $[297–497]/month | Up to 200 calls/month | Small practices, 1–2 providers |
| Growth | $[497–797]/month | Up to 500 calls/month | Medium practices, 3–5 providers |
| Scale | $[797–1,197]/month | Unlimited | Multi-provider or high-volume |

**Note:** Insert your actual prices above. The principle is:
- Starter = 1 recovered patient breaks even (low risk for them)
- Growth = 2–3 recovered patients = positive ROI
- Scale = 4–6 recovered patients = positive ROI

### Setup Fee

One-time setup: $[150–300] (covers integration, testing, first-week optimization).
Can be waived for first 10 clients as a launch incentive.

### How to Present Pricing on the Call

1. Run the Missed Call Audit first (Offer 1 — above)
2. Let them react to their own number
3. Then: "Our system at the Starter tier is $[X]/month. That's $[X/year].
   Your audit number was $[5,040]/month in potential recovered revenue.
   You'd need to recover [X price ÷ their patient value] bookings per month
   to break even. Most clients recover [3–6] in the first 60 days."
4. "Does the Starter tier make sense to start with, or do you want to
   look at Growth given your call volume?"

Let them pick the tier. Do not start with the highest price.
Let them upgrade themselves.

### Contract Terms

- Month-to-month, cancel anytime (critical for reducing close friction)
- 30-day notice to cancel (reasonable for you)
- No price lock — you reserve the right to adjust pricing with 60-day notice

## Offer 3 — Upgrade: White-Glove Setup

### What It Is

Everything in the Main Offer, plus:
- Done-for-you setup (you handle integration, no tech work for them)
- Custom call script built for their practice
- First 30 days of weekly optimization reviews (30-min calls)
- Priority support (direct access to you, not a ticketing system)

### When to Offer It

- Prospects who say: "We don't have time to manage this"
- Multi-provider or multi-location practices (more complex setup)
- Prospects who lost a significant amount to the audit number ($10K+/month)
  and have budget to match

### Pricing

$[297–497] additional per month on top of their Main Offer tier.
Or: one-time setup premium of $[500–1,000] + standard monthly rate.

### How to Position It

```
"We also have a White-Glove option where I personally handle the entire
setup — you don't touch anything technical, and we do weekly check-ins
for the first month to make sure it's working perfectly.

That's [price] more per month — about [1 additional patient value] in
terms of what you need to recover to cover it.

Some practices prefer to do it themselves once the system is running.
Others want the hand-holding upfront. Which sounds more like you?"
```

## Risk Reversal / Guarantee

### Option A — Results Guarantee (Strongest)

```
"If you don't recover at least [2] new patient bookings in your
first 30 days that you wouldn't have captured otherwise, we'll refund
your first month. No questions."
```

Use this when: you're confident in the system and need to close
a hesitant but genuinely interested prospect.
Do not overuse — only offer when the deal would otherwise be lost.

### Option B — Cancel Anytime (Standard)

```
"We work month-to-month. If after 60 days you don't feel like it's
working for you, just let us know and we'll cancel. No long-term contract."
```

Use this on every close. Removes the "what if it doesn't work" objection
before it's raised.

### Option C — Free First Week

```
"We'll get you set up and run the system live for 7 days before
your first billing date. You can evaluate it with real calls before
committing to anything."
```

Use this when: they're interested but can't commit without seeing
it work on their own practice first. Only offer if your setup is fast enough
to make a 7-day pilot meaningful.

## Offer Presentation Flow (On the Call)

```
1. Run Missed Call Audit → arrive at their specific number
2. Let them react: "That's interesting" / "That's higher than I thought"
3. Transition: "So the question is — do you want to recover that number?"
4. Yes → Present Main Offer tiers. "Most practices start at Starter.
   Given your volume, Growth might be a better fit. Here's the difference."
5. Let them pick a tier
6. Offer risk reversal: "We work month-to-month, cancel anytime."
7. Close: "Should we get you set up this week?"
8. If hesitation → "What would make this feel like an obvious yes?"
   (surfaces final objection — handle it, then re-close)
9. If yes → confirm next steps, send onboarding form within 10 minutes
```

## Pricing Objection Responses

### "That's more than I expected."

```
"I get it. Let me put the math back in front of you:
your audit number was $[X] per month in potential recovered revenue.
Our system is $[Y] per month.

You need to recover [Y÷patient value] bookings to break even.
Most practices recover [3–6] in the first 60 days.

The real risk isn't paying $[Y]. The risk is continuing to lose
$[X] every month with no fix in place."
```

### "Can I get a discount?"

```
"I don't discount the monthly rate — but I'll waive the setup fee,
which is [$Z]. That saves you [$Z] upfront with no change to what
you're getting."
```

Waiving setup is easier than discounting recurring revenue and signals
you have confidence in the monthly value.

### "Let me think about it / I need to discuss with my partner."

```
"Of course. What's the specific thing you want to think through?
I'd rather address it now than have you wrestling with it alone."
```

If they need a partner discussion:
```
"Can we get your partner on a 15-minute call? I want to make sure
they have the full picture directly rather than you having to relay
everything. When are you both available?"
```

## Onboarding: What Happens Right After the Close

Send this email within 10 minutes of close:

```
Subject: Welcome to [System Name] — next steps for {{clinic_name}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Great to have {{clinic_name}} on board.

Here's what happens next:
1. Fill in this onboarding form: [link] (takes 5–10 min)
2. I'll review your setup and send you a calendar invite
   for our setup call
3. We'll be live within [X] business days

Any questions before then, just reply here.

Looking forward to it.

[Your name]
```

Speed here signals professionalism and builds immediate confidence.
A slow onboarding email after a verbal close creates buyer's remorse.
